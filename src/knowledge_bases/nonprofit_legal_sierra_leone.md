# NON PROFIT ORGANIZATION LEGAL REQUIREMENTS IN SIERRA LEONE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## SIERRA LEONE LEGAL FRAMEWORK

### NGO Law in Sierra Leone

Primary legislation:
- NGO Act 2014 (revised)
- Societies Act
- Companies Act
- Constitution of Sierra Leone
- Registration of Societies Ordinance

Regulatory body:
- Office of the Registrar of Societies
- Ministry of Internal Affairs
- Board of Intermediate NGOs (BINGO)

---

## REGISTRATION PROCESS IN SIERRA LEONE

### Step 1: Registration as Society

Registrar of Societies:
- Office in Freetown
- Provincial offices
- Registers societies and associations

Process:
1. Prepare constitution/bylaws
2. Form founding members (minimum 3)
3. Complete registration form
4. Submit with constitution
5. Pay registration fee
6. Receive certificate

Documents needed:
- Constitution
- List of founders
- Leadership details
- Address and contact
- Mission statement

Cost: 50,000-150,000 Leones (approximately 3-9 USD)

Timeline: 2-6 weeks

---

### Step 2: Tax Registration

Sierra Leone Revenue Authority (SLRA):
- Register with SLRA
- Obtain Tax Identification Number (TIN)
- Register for VAT (if applicable)

Documentation:
- Certificate of registration
- Constitution
- Leadership information
- Tax registration form

Benefits:
- Tax exempt status (if qualified)
- Can issue tax receipts
- Credibility with government

## FINANCIAL COMPLIANCE IN SIERRA LEONE

### Accounting Requirements

Basic requirements:
- Keep financial records
- Annual accounts
- Regular bank reconciliation
- Documentation of all transactions

Reporting:
- Annual financial statement
- Filing with Registrar
- Donor reporting
- Transparency to members

Accounts:
- Separate bank account (if possible)
- Clear records (ledger, receipts)
- Regular audits (larger organizations)
- Segregation of duties

Challenges:
- Banking access limited in rural areas
- Professional accountants scarce
- Digital systems limited
- Manual record-keeping common

## PRACTICAL COSTS FOR SIERRA LEONE NGO

### Startup Costs

Registration:
- Registrar of Societies: 50,000-150,000 Leones
- Tax registration: 20,000-50,000 Leones
- Lawyer consultation: 500,000-2,000,000 Leones (if used)
- Banking (initial deposit): 1,000,000+ Leones

Total startup: 1,570,000-2,200,000 Leones (90-130 USD)

### Annual Costs

- Audit (if needed): 500,000-2,000,000 Leones
- Accounting/bookkeeping: 200,000-500,000 Leones
- Compliance: Minimal
- Renewal: Minimal

Total annual: 700,000-2,500,000 Leones (40-150 USD)
