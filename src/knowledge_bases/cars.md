# CARS ENGINE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE

### Every Month
- Check tire pressure
- Check all fluid levels (oil, coolant, brake fluid)
- Test brakes
- Check lights

### Every 6 Months (or 6,000 miles)
- Oil and filter change
- Rotate tires
- Inspect brake pads

### Every Year (or 12,000 miles)
- Professional inspection
- Check alignment
- Inspect suspension
- Check battery

### Every 2 Years
- Replace air filter
- Check transmission fluid
- Inspect belts

### Every 5 Years
- Replace brake fluid
- Flush coolant
- Deep inspection

## COMMON PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS

### Engine Won't Start
- Diagnosis:
  - Turn key - does engine crank?
  - Check battery (corrosion, connections)
  - Check starter (clicking sound?)
  - Check fuel (empty tank?)
  
- Solutions:
  - Dead battery: Jump start
  - Corroded terminals: Clean connections
  - Fuel issue: Call mechanic
  - Starter failure: Get replacement

### Strange Noises
- Grinding: Brake pads worn
- Knocking: Engine timing issue
- Squealing: Belt problem
- Clunking: Suspension/strut issue
- Clicking: CV joint

### Warning Lights
- Check Engine: Get scanned
- Oil Pressure: Low oil level
- Temperature: Overheating
- Battery: Charging system issue
- Brake: Low pad/fluid

### Tire Issues
- Uneven wear: Alignment needed
- Bulges: Replace immediately
- Punctures: Patch or replace
- Bald tires: Safety hazard

## CAR BUYING ADVICE

### New vs Used
**New:**
- Warranty (3-5 years)
- Latest tech
- Most expensive
- Depreciation: 20% first year

**Used:**
- No warranty (usually)
- Older tech
- Cheaper
- Depreciation done

### What to Check
- Maintenance history
- Accident report (Carfax)
- Test drive conditions
- Mechanic pre-purchase inspection
- Price comparisons

### Negotiation
- Know market price
- Get pre-approved financing
- Timing: End of month/quarter
- Walk away if price too high
- Get everything in writing
