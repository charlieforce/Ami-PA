# FundiConnect - COMPREHENSIVE

## Platform Overview

**Entity Type:** Progressive Web App (PWA) Two-Sided Marketplace
**Market:** Nairobi, Kenya
**Problem:** Artisans lack customers; customers lack trustworthy providers
**Solution:** Tech-enabled marketplace with trust mechanisms and payment escrow

## Business Model: Deposit-Plus-Site-Payment

**Phase 1: Booking & Deposit**
- Customer submits service request
- Artisan quotes and customer accepts
- Customer pays deposit (20-40%) through platform
- Deposit held in escrow
- Service confirmed and scheduled

**Phase 2: Service Completion & Full Payment**
- Artisan completes work
- Customer inspects and verifies quality
- Customer releases full payment
- Platform processes payment to artisan
- Transaction complete

**Why This Works:**
- Trust: Money held safely in escrow
- Quality: Customer approval required before payment
- Artisan Security: Payment guarantee upon completion
- Quick Liquidity: Paid within 24-48 hours
- Dispute Resolution: Mechanism for unresolved issues

**Revenue Model:** 7-15% commission per transaction
