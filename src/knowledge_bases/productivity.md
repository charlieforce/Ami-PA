# PRODUCTIVITY ENGINE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## FRAMEWORKS

### Getting Things Done (GTD)
1. Capture - Get everything out of your head
2. Clarify - Decide if it's actionable
3. Organize - Put it in right system
4. Reflect - Weekly review
5. Engage - Do the work

### Eisenhower Matrix
- Urgent/Important: DO FIRST
- Important/Not Urgent: SCHEDULE
- Urgent/Not Important: DELEGATE
- Neither: ELIMINATE

### Pomodoro Technique
- 25 minutes focused work
- 5 minute break
- After 4 pomodoros: 15 min break
- No distractions during focus time

### Time Blocking
- Calendar your time
- Deep work blocks (uninterrupted)
- Admin blocks
- Break blocks
- Meeting blocks

### Goal Setting

#### SMART Goals
- Specific: Exactly what
- Measurable: How to track
- Achievable: Realistic
- Relevant: Matters to you
- Time-bound: By when

#### OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)
- Objective: Qualitative goal
- Key Results: 3-5 measurable outcomes
- Ambitious but achievable
- Quarterly focus

### Habit Building
- Start small (2 minute habit)
- Habit stacking (attach to existing)
- Track daily
- Build in 30 days
- Reward completion
