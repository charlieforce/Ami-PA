## HAIR TYPES & MAINTENANCE IN SIERRA LEONE

### Natural Hair

Type:
- Coily, textured hair
- Requires moisture
- Prone to breakage
- High maintenance

Care requirements:
- Regular conditioning
- Protective styling
- Deep treatments
- Scalp care
- Products (local and imported)

Services:
- Wash and set
- Braiding (cornrows, box braids)
- Twists and locs
- Hair treatments
- Scalp treatments

Products needed:
- Shampoos (sulfate-free)
- Conditioners (deep and regular)
- Oils and butters
- Hair masks
- Styling products

---

### Relaxed Hair

Trend:
- Still common in Sierra Leone
- Chemical relaxers used
- Professional application needed
- Regular touch-ups (6-8 weeks)

Services:
- Hair relaxing
- Touch-ups
- Conditioning treatments
- Color/highlights
- Straightening

Products:
- Relaxer creams
- Neutralizing shampoos
- Deep conditioners
- Styling products
- Protective treatments

Safety considerations:
- Chemical burns risk
- Hair damage from improper application
- Scalp health
- Professional training needed
- Proper ventilation

---

### Weaves & Extensions

Popularity:
- Very popular in Freetown

- Various types available
- Regular maintenance needed

Types:
- Hair extensions (attach to natural hair)

## SALON BUSINESS MODEL

### Startup Costs (Salon)

Equipment:
- Salon chairs: 1,000,000-2,000,000 Leones each (3-5 chairs)
- Wash basins: 500,000-1,000,000 Leones each
- Mirrors: 200,000-500,000 Leones each
- Shelving/storage: 300,000-800,000 Leones
- Hair dryers: 200,000-500,000 Leones each

Location:
- Rent: 2,000,000-10,000,000 Leones/month (Freetown)
- Utilities: 500,000-1,000,000 Leones/month
- Renovation: 2,000,000-5,000,000 Leones

Products:
- Initial inventory: 2,000,000-5,000,000 Leones
- Supplies: 500,000-1,000,000 Leones/month

Staff:
- Stylist salary: 500,000-1,500,000 Leones/month per person
- Assistant salary: 300,000-800,000 Leones/month

Total startup: 15,000,000-40,000,000 Leones (900-2,400 USD)

### Revenue Model

Per service pricing (typical Freetown rates):
- Wash and set: 15,000-50,000 Leones
- Braiding (per hour): 30,000-100,000 Leones
- Hair relaxing: 50,000-150,000 Leones
- Extensions/weaves: 100,000-300,000 Leones
- Conditioning treatments: 30,000-80,000 Leones

Monthly revenue potential:
- With 1 stylist: 2,000,000-5,000,000 Leones/month
- With 3 stylists: 5,000,000-15,000,000 Leones/month
- With 5 stylists: 10,000,000-25,000,000 Leones/month
