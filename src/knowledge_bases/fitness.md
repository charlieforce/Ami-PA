# FITNESS & NUTRITION ENGINE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## TRAINING TYPES

### Strength Training
- Builds muscle
- Increases metabolism
- Strengthens bones
- 3-4 days per week
- Major compound movements

### Cardio
- Improves heart health
- Burns calories
- Increases endurance
- 150 min moderate per week
- Many options (running, cycling, swimming)

### Flexibility
- Maintains range of motion
- Prevents injury
- Reduces tension
- Daily practice
- Yoga, stretching

## NUTRITION BASICS

### Macronutrients
- **Protein**: Muscle building, recovery (1g per lb bodyweight)
- **Carbs**: Energy, athletic performance
- **Fats**: Hormone production, brain health

### Micronutrients
- Vitamins: Essential for body functions
- Minerals: Bone health, immunity
- Hydration: Critical for everything

### Calorie Balance
- Surplus: Build muscle
- Maintenance: Sustain
- Deficit: Lose fat
- TDEE = Total Daily Energy Expenditure

## COMMON GOALS

### Lose Fat
- Calorie deficit (500 below TDEE)
- Maintain protein
- Strength training
- Cardio added
- Patience (1-2 lbs per week)

### Build Muscle
- Calorie surplus (500 above TDEE)
- Progressive overload
- High protein
- Adequate carbs for energy
- Recovery matters
