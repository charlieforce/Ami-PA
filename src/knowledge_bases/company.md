# Charlie's Companies & Ventures

## The Ecosystem Vision

Charlie operates across **4 major ventures** that work together as an ecosystem:

1. **GII (Nonprofit)** - Free tech education across Africa
2. **GII Connect (For-Profit)** - EdTech platform for commercial scaling
3. **FundiConnect (For-Profit)** - Artisan marketplace in Nairobi
4. **TechieVet (For-Profit)** - AI PM tool for tech teams
5. **Promoga (For-Profit)** - Fitness marketplace in Toronto

**Model:** Nonprofit provides impact; for-profits generate revenue to fund nonprofit. Sustainable social entrepreneurship.

---

## GII Quick Facts
- **Type:** U.S. 501(c)(3) Nonprofit
- **Founded:** January 4, 2025
- **Mission:** Free tech education across Africa
- **Current Reach:** 3,348+ learners across 7 countries
- **Key Achievement:** First mandatory tech curriculum in Sierra Leone (2 schools, 1,000 students)
- **Team:** Charlie (founder), 16 certified teachers
- **Funding:** Grants + GII Connect revenue + partnerships

## GII Connect Quick Facts
- **Type:** For-Profit EdTech
- **Purpose:** Commercial platform to fund GII
- **Features:** AI tutoring in African languages, mentorship, opportunity matching
- **Tech Stack:** React (Jackson), Node.js/Express (Nelson)
- **Revenue Model:** NGO white-label licensing + subscriptions
- **Target:** 10+ organizational partners by 2027

## FundiConnect Quick Facts
- **Type:** For-Profit Marketplace PWA
- **Market:** Nairobi, Kenya
- **Problem:** Artisans need customers; customers need trustworthy service providers
- **Services:** Plumbing, electrical, carpentry, home maintenance, etc.
- **Model:** Deposit-plus-payment with platform commission (7-15%)
- **Team:** Jackson (frontend), Nelson (backend)
- **Launch:** Post-testing phase (mid-2026)
- **Target:** 500+ artisans, 10,000+ customers by 2027

## TechieVet Quick Facts
- **Type:** For-Profit SaaS PM Tool
- **Problem:** Tech teams waste time on status rollups, miss risks
- **Solution:** AI-powered insights for project managers
- **Tech Stack:** React (Charlie), Python/Flask (Mo)
- **Features:** Bottleneck detection, risk identification, automated reporting
- **AI Integration:** Claude for intelligent insights
- **Status:** Alpha development, backend updates pending
- **Target:** 500 teams by end of 2027

---

## Promoga Quick Facts
- **Type:** For-Profit Marketplace
- **Market:** Toronto, Canada (fitness studios & instructors)
- **Problem:** Fitness industry is fragmented; no integrated system
- **Solution:** Unified platform for studios, instructors, clients
- **Tech Stack:** React (Thanuganesh), A/B testing via PostHog
- **Go-to-Market:** Partnership with Canadian Yoga Alliance (CYA)
- **Revenue Model:** Studio subscriptions + commission
- **Target:** 200+ studios, 50K+ clients by 2027
