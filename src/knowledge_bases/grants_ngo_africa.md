# GRANTS FOR NGOS IN AFRICA KNOWLEDGE BASE

## AFRICAN GRANT LANDSCAPE

### Overview

Funding sources:
- International foundations
- Bilateral donors (government to government)
- Multilateral organizations (UN, World Bank, etc.)
- International NGOs
- Corporate foundations
- Local/diaspora funding (growing)
- Government grants (limited)

Characteristics:
- Heavy reliance on international donors
- Complex reporting requirements
- Competition high
- Timeline longer
- Capacity challenges
- Currency and banking issues

## COMPETITIVE GRANT PROCESSES

### RFP (Request for Proposal)

Timeline:
- Posted on websites/email
- 2-4 week application window
- Highly competitive
- Evaluation takes 1-2 months
- Award notification

Process:
1. Identify opportunity
2. Assess fit
3. Develop proposal (7-10 days)
4. Internal review
5. Submit before deadline
6. Wait for notification

---

### Application Requirements

Typical needs:
- Organizational capacity assessment
- Financial statements (3 years)
- Board documentation
- Program experience
- M&E framework
- Budget (detailed)
- Letters of support
- References

Complexity:
- 30-50 pages typical
- Multiple documents
- Detailed budgets
- Sustainability plan
- Risk assessment
- Timeline

## UNIQUE AFRICAN CHALLENGES

### Banking and Payments

Challenge:
- Limited access to international banking
- Currency controls
- High transaction costs
- Slow transfers

Solutions:
- Build banking relationships
- Use local partners
- Plan for delays
- Budget for exchange rates
- Multiple bank accounts

---

### Capacity Building

Challenge:
- Limited financial management expertise
- Accounting systems weak
- Audit availability limited
- Record-keeping challenges

Solutions:
- Invest in capacity
- Hire finance staff
- Use accounting software
- Build systems before fundraising
- Partner with established NGOs

---

### Currency Volatility

Challenge:
- Exchange rates fluctuate
- Affects budget reality
- Operational challenges

Solutions:
- Build flexibility into budget
- Use local currency where possible
- Forward planning
- Exchange rate assumptions clear

---

### Reporting Burden

Challenge:
- Complex donor requirements
- Multiple donors = multiple reports
- English often required
- Time-intensive

Solutions:
- Centralize data collection
- Use shared systems
- Consolidate reports where possible
- Quality reporting staff

## GRANT PROPOSAL TIPS FOR AFRICA

### Context Matters

Emphasize:
- Local ownership
- Community involvement
- Sustainability plan
- Government alignment
- Partnership approach
- Scalability potential
- Cost-effectiveness
- Impact on marginalized

---

### Problem Statement

Strong:
- Concrete statistics (use data)
- Local research
- Community perspective
- Why current approach inadequate
- Opportunity for change
- Urgency clear

---

### Implementation

Show:
- Local team capacity
- Track record
- Clear timeline
- Risk management
- Contingency planning
- Local partnership
- Sustainability beyond grant

---

## BEST PRACTICES

1. Capacity first: Build before aggressive fundraising
2. Relationships: Meet funders before asking
3. Alignment: Show clear mission match
4. Quality: Excellent proposal writing
5. Sustainability: Plan beyond donor funding
6. Reporting: Track and report rigorously
7. Gratitude: Acknowledge funders genuinely
8. Transparency: Open about challenges and failures
9. Learning: Show how you improve
10. Persistence: Keep applying despite rejections
