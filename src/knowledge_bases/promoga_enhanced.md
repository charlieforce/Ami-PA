# Promoga Studio 360 - COMPREHENSIVE

## Platform Overview

**Entity Type:** For-Profit Fitness Marketplace
**Market:** Toronto, Canada (Greater Toronto Area)
**Focus:** Fitness studios, instructors, and wellness enthusiasts
**Problem:** Fragmented fitness industry with poor tools and discovery
**Solution:** Unified marketplace connecting studios, instructors, clients

## Market Analysis

**For Fitness Studios:**
- Class scheduling across multiple platforms
- Instructor management and payment complexity
- Student data scattered across systems
- Limited marketing tools
- High operational overhead

**For Fitness Instructors:**
- No integrated platform for class management
- Payment processing nightmare
- Scheduling conflicts and double-booking
- Difficulty building personal brand
- Payment delays and processing fees

**For Clients/Students:**
- Difficult to discover quality classes and instructors
- Multiple apps to manage
- No unified class calendar
- Inconsistent instructor information
- Confusing pricing structures

**Market Size:** Toronto fitness market $2B+, GTA 6M+ people, 15%+ fitness participation = 900K+ potential users

## Regulatory & Compliance

**CASL Compliance (Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation):**
- Email marketing properly configured
- Unsubscribe link in every email
- Clear sender identification
- 30-day response requirement
- Penalties: $1-10M+ for violations

**PIPEDA Compliance (Personal Information Protection & Electronic Documents Act):**
- Data privacy and security measures implemented
- Clear privacy policy
- Secure data collection and storage
- Data breach notification (within 30 days)
- Penalties: $5K+ per violation
