# Global Impact Innovators (GII)
## The Nonprofit Core Mission

### What Is GII?
Global Impact Innovators is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN: 99-3205882) founded by Charles Bond Kebbi in 2025. GII delivers free technology and entrepreneurship education across Africa, with operations in West Africa (Sierra Leone) and East Africa (Kenya).

**Vision:** A thriving, inclusive tech ecosystem across Africa where women and young entrepreneurs in underserved communities have equal access to education, mentorship, and opportunities to create positive change and sustainable innovation.

**Mission:** Empowering African entrepreneurs, particularly women and those in rural areas, by providing free, accessible tech startup and entrepreneurship training.

### Why It Matters
- Technology education is a gatekeeping function in Africa
- Talented entrepreneurs lack access to foundational knowledge
- Women and rural populations are systematically excluded
- One founder can create 10+ jobs and lift entire communities
- Africa's demographics: 60% under 25, massive potential untapped

### The Model
**Free = Inclusive**
- No barriers to entry
- Serving those who can't afford commercial programs
- Building sustainable change through education, not handouts

**Tech + Entrepreneurship**
- Digital literacy foundations
- Coding and AI education
- Startup fundamentals
- Business planning
- Pitch preparation

**Local + Scalable**
- Delivered in local languages (Krio, Mende, Temne, Luo, Swahili)
- Trained local instructors
- Community partnerships
- Designed for rapid replication

### Historic Milestone: Mandatory School Adoption
**First in Sierra Leone:**
Two schools now require GII curriculum as mandatory coursework:
- **St. Andrew's Secondary School** (~500 science students) - Started May 6, 2026
- **United Brethren in Christ SSS** (~500 students)

Combined Impact: ~1,000 students now have mandatory tech education embedded in their curriculum.

This is the first time in Sierra Leone's educational history that a tech curriculum has been made mandatory. It proves that:
- GII's curriculum works
- Schools see the value
- The model is scalable
- Quality is recognized by government partners
