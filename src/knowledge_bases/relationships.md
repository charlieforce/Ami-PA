# RELATIONSHIPS ENGINE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## COMMUNICATION PRINCIPLES

### Active Listening
- Focus fully on the person
- Don't interrupt
- Reflect back what you hear
- Ask clarifying questions
- Validate feelings

### Conflict Resolution
1. Listen without defending
2. Find common ground
3. State your perspective calmly
4. Propose solutions together
5. Follow up after resolution

### Setting Boundaries
- Be clear about limits
- Explain why it matters to you
- Offer alternatives
- Be consistent
- Respect their boundaries

## RELATIONSHIP TYPES

### Friendships
- Regular contact matters
- Show you care (check-ins)
- Be reliable
- Celebrate wins
- Support through challenges

### Family
- Communicate regularly
- Respect differences
- Set healthy boundaries
- Show appreciation
- Address conflicts directly

### Romantic Relationships
- Communicate openly
- Show appreciation daily
- Handle conflicts respectfully
- Make time together
- Individual growth space

### Professional Relationships
- Be reliable
- Communicate clearly
- Respect boundaries
- Celebrate others' wins
- Address issues early

## RED FLAGS

### Toxic Signs
- Constant criticism
- Manipulation
- Lying/dishonesty
- Disrespecting boundaries
- Making you feel bad
- One-sided effort

### Healthy Signs
- Mutual respect
- Open communication
- Trust and honesty
- Support each other
- Balanced effort
- Feel valued
