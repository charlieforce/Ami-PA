# CAREER ENGINE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## INTERVIEW PREPARATION

### Common Questions
- Tell me about yourself
- Why this company
- Why this role
- Biggest challenge
- Failure experience
- Strengths/weaknesses
- Where do you see yourself in 5 years

### STAR Method
- **Situation**: Context
- **Task**: What you needed to do
- **Action**: What you did
- **Result**: What happened

### Salary Negotiation
- Research market rate
- Know your value
- Get offer in writing
- Negotiate respectfully
- Consider total package
- Know your walk-away number

## RESUME TIPS

### Structure
- Contact info
- Professional summary
- Experience (reverse chronological)
- Education
- Skills
- Certifications

### Content
- Quantify achievements
- Use action verbs
- Highlight impact
- Tailor to job
- Keep to 1-2 pages

## LINKEDIN OPTIMIZATION

### Profile
- Professional photo
- Compelling headline
- Detailed summary
- Rich experience section
- Skills/endorsements
- Recommendations

### Networking
- Connect strategically
- Personalized messages
- Engage with content
- Share thoughts
- Build community
- Help others first
