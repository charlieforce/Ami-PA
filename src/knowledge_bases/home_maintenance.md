# HOME MAINTENANCE ENGINE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## ELECTRICAL

### Common Issues
- Tripped circuit breakers
  - Solution: Check what's overloaded, reset breaker
  - When to call pro: If it keeps tripping
  
- Flickering lights
  - Solution: Check bulb connection, replace bulb
  - When to call pro: If all lights flicker (wiring issue)
  
- Dead outlets
  - Solution: Check breaker, test outlet
  - When to call pro: Multiple dead outlets
  
- Burning smell from outlets
  - Solution: STOP - Turn off power immediately
  - When to call pro: IMMEDIATELY - Fire hazard

### Maintenance
- Check outlets monthly
- Replace damaged cords
- Don't overload outlets
- Test GFCI outlets monthly (bathroom, kitchen)

## PLUMBING

### Common Issues
- Clogged drains
  - Solution: Use plunger or drain snake
  - DIY: Baking soda + vinegar
  - When to call pro: If multiple drains clogged
  
- Leaky faucets
  - Solution: Replace washers/cartridge
  - When to call pro: If internal parts damaged
  
- Running toilet
  - Solution: Check flapper, replace if worn
  - DIY: $15-30 fix
  - When to call pro: If tank issues
  
- Burst pipes
  - Solution: SHUT OFF WATER immediately
  - When to call pro: IMMEDIATELY - Water damage risk

### Maintenance
- Check under sinks monthly
- Clean drain regularly
- Know where water shutoff is
- Prevent frozen pipes in winter

## GENERAL REPAIRS

### Drywall
- Small holes: Spackle + sand
- Large holes: Patch + paint
- When to call pro: Water damage, large areas

### Painting
- Prep: Clean, prime
- Paint: Multiple thin coats
- Tools: Roller, brush, drop cloth
- DIY cost: Cheap
- Pro cost: Higher but perfect finish

### Doors/Windows
- Stuck doors: Check hinges, sand edges
- Broken seals: Replace weatherstripping
- When to call pro: Glass repair, frame damage

## APPLIANCE MAINTENANCE

### AC Unit
- Monthly: Change filter
- Quarterly: Professional check-up
- Yearly: Deep cleaning
- Signs of failure: Strange noises, weak cooling

### Water Heater
- Flush yearly
- Check temperature 120°F
- Lifespan: 8-12 years
- When to replace: Rust, leaking

### Washer/Dryer
- Clean dryer vent monthly
- Check hoses for leaks
- Don't overload
- Use lint trap
