# Promoga Studio 360
## Fitness Instructor & Studio Marketplace

### What Is Promoga Studio 360?
Promoga is a **marketplace connecting fitness studios and instructors with clients** in the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), Canada.

**Market:** Toronto, Canada
**Website:** promoga.ca or promogastudio.ca
**Status:** Active product development with go-to-market preparation

### The Problem Promoga Solves

**For Fitness Studios:**
- Scheduling and student management fragmented across multiple tools
- Class coordination with independent instructors is chaotic
- Payment processing split between multiple platforms
- No integrated student retention tools
- Hard to scale from small studio to enterprise

**For Fitness Instructors:**
- Teaching multiple studios means juggling multiple systems
- No way to build own following independent of studio
- Payment flows are slow and unreliable
- Marketing is DIY and inefficient
- Difficulty scaling income

**For Clients:**
- Finding quality classes and instructors is hard
- No centralized booking system
- Payment hassles (class passes, drop-ins, memberships)
- Discovery of new studios/instructors is limited
- No trusted review system

### Market Opportunity

**Fitness Industry Scale:**
- GTA population: 6M+
- 2M+ fitness enthusiasts
- $800M+ annual spending on fitness
- 4,000+ studios and independent instructors
- Massive fragmentation and inefficiency

**Promoga's Wedge:**
Build the operational backbone for the fitness industry - the system everything plugs into.

### Business Model

**Three-sided marketplace:**
1. **Studios** - Pay for platform + commission on classes
2. **Instructors** - Sell classes independently or with studios
3. **Clients** - Pay for classes directly through platform

**Revenue Model:**
- Studio subscriptions: $200-$500/month based on tier
- Commission on classes: 5-10%
- Instructor tools (premium): $10-$20/month
- Premium features: analytics, marketing, advanced scheduling

**Unit Economics:**
- Cost per customer acquired: $5-$15
- Lifetime value per studio: $2,000-$5,000
- Lifetime value per instructor: $500-$1,500
- Gross margin: 70%+
- Path to profitability clear

### Competitive Landscape Analysis

**Benchmark Platforms Studied:**

**Acuity Scheduling:**
- Largest player in this space
- Strong on scheduling, weak on community
- High customer acquisition cost
- Promoga can out-compete on UX and integrations

**Momence:**
- Marketplace for fitness services
- Beautiful UI but limited instructor tools
- Promoga can offer better instructor economics

**Strategy Derived:**
- Lesson 1: Strong UX matters more than features
- Lesson 2: Instructor retention is critical
- Lesson 3: Communities are powerful growth drivers
- Lesson 4: Payment processing is table-stakes
