# MECHANICAL & AUTOMOTIVE EXPERTISE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## FUNDAMENTALS

### Engine Basics
Internal Combustion Engine: Fuel burns to create power

Four-Stroke Cycle (most common):
1. Intake: Air/fuel enters cylinder
2. Compression: Piston compresses mixture
3. Combustion: Spark ignites fuel, explosion pushes piston
4. Exhaust: Burnt gases exit

Key Components:
- Cylinder: Where combustion happens
- Piston: Moves up/down from combustion
- Crankshaft: Converts up/down motion to rotation
- Valves: Control intake/exhaust
- Spark plug: Ignites fuel

---

### Engine Types

Gasoline (Petrol):
- Most common
- Quick acceleration
- Higher emissions
- More maintenance

Diesel:
- Better fuel efficiency
- More torque
- Longer engine life
- Higher cost
- Slower acceleration

Hybrid:
- Gas + electric motor
- Electric assists/regenerates
- Best fuel efficiency
- Higher cost, complex
- Growing trend

Electric:
- Zero emissions
- Silent operation
- Lower maintenance
- Limited range
- Charging time
- Future direction

## BASIC CAR MAINTENANCE

### Oil Changes
Purpose: Lubricate engine, remove dirt

Frequency: Every 3,000-10,000 miles (depends on synthetic)

Steps:
1. Warm up engine
2. Drain old oil from plug
3. Replace oil filter
4. Add new oil
5. Check level with dipstick

Cost: $25-75 DIY, $50-150 professional

---

### Air Filter Replacement
Purpose: Prevent dirt entering engine

Frequency: Every 15,000-30,000 miles

Signs it's dirty:
- Reduced fuel economy
- Poor acceleration
- Engine knocking

Cost: $15-50

---

### Tire Maintenance
Tire Pressure: Check monthly
- Under-inflated: Worse fuel economy, tire damage
- Over-inflated: Poor handling, blowout risk
- Correct: Better fuel economy, longer life

Tire Rotation: Every 5,000-8,000 miles
- Promotes even wear
- Extends tire life

Tire Replacement: When tread reaches 2/32 inch
- Use penny test: Insert penny upside down, if top of Lincoln visible, replace
- Cost: $50-200 per tire

---

### Brake System

Components:
- Brake pads: Friction material (wears over time)
- Brake rotor/disc: Metal disc pads squeeze
- Brake fluid: Transfers force
- Brake lines: Carries brake fluid
- Calipers: Apply pads to rotor

Warning Signs:
- Squeaking/grinding noise
- Soft brake pedal
- Vibration when braking
- Warning light on dashboard

Maintenance:
- Replace pads when worn (every 50,000-70,000 miles)
- Bleed brake fluid every 2 years
- Cost: $150-400 per axle

---

### Battery

Purpose: Provides electrical power to start engine

Lifespan: 3-5 years typically

Signs of failure:
- Slow cranking
- Dim lights
- No power/clicking sound
- Corroded terminals

Maintenance:
- Clean corrosion (white/blue crusty stuff)
- Ensure tight connections
- Replace when it dies
- Cost: $100-300

---

### Coolant/Antifreeze

Purpose: Cools engine, prevents freezing

Maintenance:
- Check level monthly
- Flush and replace every 30,000-50,000 miles
- Correct type matters (not all coolants compatible)
- Never open hot radiator cap (burns!)

Signs of problem:
- Overheating gauge high
- Sweet smell (leak)
- White steam from hood
- Puddle under car

---

### Transmission

Purpose: Delivers engine power to wheels at different speeds

Types:
- Manual: Driver controls gear shifts
- Automatic: Car shifts gears automatically
- CVT: Continuously variable (smooth acceleration)

Maintenance:
- Check fluid level
- Replace fluid every 60,000-100,000 miles
- Avoid aggressive driving
- Cost to replace: $1,500-3,000+

## COMMON PROBLEMS

### Problem 1: Check Engine Light

Means: Onboard diagnostics detected problem

Severity: Usually not immediate emergency, but needs fixing

Diagnosis:
- Use OBD-2 scanner ($25-50) to read code
- Common codes: P0301 (misfire), P0420 (catalyst system), P0505 (idle)

Solutions:
- Tighten gas cap (sometimes this is it!)
- Check for loose parts
- Replace faulty sensor
- Have mechanic read full diagnostic

---

### Problem 2: Overheating

Symptoms:
- Temperature gauge in red
- Steam from hood
- Sweet smell

Causes:
- Low coolant
- Failed thermostat
- Water pump failure
- Radiator clog
- Fan not working

Immediate action:
- Pull over safely
- Turn off air conditioning
- Turn on heater (pulls heat away from engine)
- Turn off engine and let cool
- Do NOT open radiator cap while hot

Solutions:
- Add coolant (if low)
- Replace thermostat ($150-300)
- Repair water pump ($300-800)
- Clean radiator

---

### Problem 3: Strange Noises

Grinding: Metal on metal, usually brakes need replacement

Knocking: Engine knock, usually bad fuel or carbon buildup
- Add quality fuel
- Use premium if recommended
- Carbon cleaner additive ($10-20)

Squealing: Belt is slipping
- Check belt tension
- Replace if cracked ($50-200)

Clunking: Suspension or engine mount issue
- Have mechanic check
- Usually suspension repair ($200-1,000)

---

### Problem 4: Fluid Leaks

What color leak:
- Red: Transmission or power steering
- Green/Orange: Coolant
- Black/Brown: Oil
- Clear: Air conditioning condensation (normal)

Diagnosis:
- Place cardboard under car overnight
- Identify color and smell
- Check levels

Solution:
- Low level: Top off immediately
- Visible leak: Find source
- Replace seals/hoses ($100-500)

---

## SUSPENSION SYSTEM

Components:
- Shocks/Struts: Absorb bumps
- Springs: Support car weight
- Control arms: Connect wheel to frame
- Sway bar: Reduces body roll

Signs of wear:
- Rough ride over bumps
- Squeaking sounds
- Car leans in turns
- Uneven tire wear
- Nose dives when braking

Maintenance:
- Replace shocks every 50,000-100,000 miles
- Alignment every 2 years or if pulling
- Cost: $400-1,500 for complete overhaul

---

## PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE

Every Month:
- Check all fluid levels
- Inspect tires and pressure
- Test lights and wipers
- Check brakes

Every 6 Months:
- Rotate tires
- Visual inspection underneath
- Check battery terminals

Every Year:
- Full inspection
- Wheel alignment
- Brake inspection
- Filter check

Every 2 Years:
- Transmission fluid top-up
- Brake fluid bleed
- AC service

Every 3-5 Years:
- Major fluid replacements
- Deep cleaning
- Professional diagnostics
