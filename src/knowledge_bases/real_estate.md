# REAL ESTATE ENGINE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## HOME BUYING PROCESS

### Steps
1. Get pre-approved
2. Find real estate agent
3. Search properties
4. Make offer
5. Get inspection
6. Appraisal
7. Final walkthrough
8. Close

### Costs
- Down payment (3-20%)
- Closing costs (2-5%)
- Inspection ($300-700)
- Appraisal ($400-600)
- Mortgage insurance (if <20%)

## FINANCING

### Mortgage Basics
- 30-year (lower payment, more interest)
- 15-year (higher payment, less interest)
- Interest rate
- Fixed vs adjustable
- Pre-approval needed

### Down Payment
- 3% minimum (FHA)
- 5% conventional
- 10-20% traditional
- Larger = lower mortgage insurance

## RENTAL CONSIDERATIONS

### Finding Place
- Budget (30% of income)
- Location research
- Commute time
- Neighborhood feel
- Safety rating

### Lease Negotiation
- Read carefully
- Understand terms
- Pet policies
- Maintenance responsibility
- Break clauses
