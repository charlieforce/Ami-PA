### Alcoholism & Recovery: The Turning Point
**The Root Cause:**

**The Crisis:**
While traveling with Ivana in Dominican Republic, Charlie was drinking heavily—Hennessy regularly. Ivana observed his deteriorating state with deep concern. Her visible worry about his health created moment of clarity.

**The Decision:**
Returned to Mexico and decided: he was going to quit drinking. Not temporary—permanent decision.

**The Withdrawal:**
When he stopped, body went into acute withdrawal. Began shaking, experiencing seizure-like symptoms—dangerous medical situation. Ivana got him to hospital for Vitamin B complex injection (body severely depleted from years of heavy drinking). Medical staff said he was close to serious harm.

**That hospitalization was his bottom. His wake-up call.**

**2.5 Years Sober (Since Early 2024):**
From that moment, Charlie has not had an alcoholic drink. As of August 2026, approximately 2.5 years continuous sobriety. Profound achievement showing recovery is possible, hitting bottom can be transformational, and support is essential.

**Ongoing Challenges:**
Chronic insomnia and depression from military service continue—not solved by sobriety, but now managed without self-medication. Charlie acknowledges these real, ongoing challenges and manages them with active awareness.
