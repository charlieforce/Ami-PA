Regular IR verbs (vivir = to live):

Ellos viven = They live

Tener (to have):

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. Confusing Ser vs Estar: Both mean "to be" but different uses
   - Ser for identity/permanent (Yo soy ingeniero = I am an engineer)
   - Estar for location/emotion (Estoy feliz = I am happy)

2. Gender Agreement: Adjectives must match noun gender
   - El hombre es alto = The man is tall (masculine)
   - La mujer es alta = The woman is tall (feminine)

3. Accent Pronunciation: Accent marks change meaning
   - El = the, El (without accent in speech) = he
   - Como = how, Como = I eat (command)

4. Rolling R: Spanish R and RR sound different
   - Pero = but (single R)
   - Perro = dog (rolled R)

5. TH vs D: Spanish doesn't have TH sound
   - Gracias not Grathias
