# BUSINESS MANAGEMENT EXPERTISE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## MANAGEMENT FUNDAMENTALS

### What is Management?
Definition: Getting things done through other people

Core Functions:
1. Planning: Set goals and strategy
2. Organizing: Structure and resources
3. Leading: Inspire and direct people
4. Controlling: Monitor and adjust

Leadership vs Management:
- Management: Doing things right (efficiency)
- Leadership: Doing right things (effectiveness)
- Need both: Efficient execution of right strategy

## ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

### Functional Organization
Structure: By function (Sales, Engineering, Marketing, Operations)

Pros:
- Clear specialization
- Deep expertise
- Easier to scale single function
- Clear career paths

Cons:
- Communication silos
- Slow decision-making
- Functional conflicts
- Less customer focus

Best for: Larger, stable companies

### Product-Based Organization
Structure: By product line (Product A team, Product B team)

Pros:
- Each team owns end-to-end
- Faster decisions
- Clear accountability
- Customer focused

Cons:
- Duplication of roles
- Harder to scale specialized function
- Coordination challenges

Best for: Multi-product companies (your situation!)

### Matrix Organization
Structure: Hybrid (functional + product/project)

Example: Engineer reports to Engineering lead AND Product team lead

Pros:
- Best of both worlds
- Flexibility
- Shared resources
- Innovation

Cons:
- Complex (who do I report to?)
- Conflict potential
- Communication heavy
- Overhead

Best for: Large, complex organizations

## COMMUNICATION

### Internal Communication

Alignment Meetings:
- Daily standup: 15 min, what did you do, what's next, blockers?
- Weekly team meeting: Goals, progress, priorities
- Monthly all-hands: Company goals, wins, challenges
- Quarterly planning: Strategy, goals, resources

One-on-ones:
- Weekly 30-60 min with direct reports
- Discuss progress, challenges, growth
- Safe space for concerns
- Build relationship

Communication Channels:
- Synchronous: Meetings, calls (real-time but interrupt)
- Asynchronous: Email, Slack, docs (flexible but slower)
- Balance needed: Async default, sync when needed

### External Communication

Investor updates:
- Monthly or quarterly
- Clear metrics and narrative
- Problems and plan to solve
- Ask if need support

Customer communication:
- Regular check-ins
- Feedback loops
- Transparency on issues
- Value demonstration

Press/Media:
- Consistent messaging
- Prepared announcements
- Crisis communication plan
- Brand voice

## DECISION-MAKING

### Decision Frameworks

RACI Matrix:
- R (Responsible): Does the work
- A (Accountable): Final decision maker
- C (Consulted): Gives input
- I (Informed): Told of decision

Example:
- Feature decision: PM accountable, Engineers responsible, Design consulted, Leadership informed

Decision Levels:
- Type 1: Reversible, fast, delegate, learn
- Type 2: Irreversible, slow, careful, data-driven
- Type 3: Urgent but reversible, decide with 70% info

Decision Process:
1. Define the question clearly
2. Gather relevant information
3. Consider options
4. Evaluate pros/cons
5. Decide based on data + intuition
6. Communicate decision and rationale
7. Implement and monitor
8. Learn from outcome

## PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

### Goal Setting (OKRs)

Quarterly Process:
- Month 1: Draft goals based on strategy
- Month 1: Team input and alignment
- Month 1: Finalize and commit
- Months 2-3: Execute and monitor
- Month 3: Review and reflect

Characteristics of Good Goals:
- Ambitious but achievable
- Measurable
- Time-bound
- Aligned with company mission
- Clear ownership

### One-on-One Meetings

Structure (30-60 min, weekly):
- Employee updates: What they did, what's next
- Feedback: Positive and constructive
- Growth: Development, learning, opportunities
- Wellbeing: How are they doing?
- Q&A: What do they need?

Tips:
- Regular schedule (same time weekly)
- Employee-led (they set agenda)
- Private and confidential
- Take notes
- Follow through on commitments

### Performance Reviews

Frequency: Quarterly or annual

Components:
- Self-assessment: Employee reflection
- Manager assessment: Performance against goals
- Feedback: 360 degree if possible
- Calibration: Fair rating across team
- Development plan: Growth areas

Conversation:
- Start positive
- Specific examples
- Clear expectations
- Growth opportunities
- Compensation discussion
- Career path

Ratings:
- Exceeds expectations: Top performers
- Meets expectations: Solid contributors
- Needs improvement: Support/coaching
- Underperforming: Performance plan or exit

## TEAM DYNAMICS

### Psychological Safety
Definition: Ability to take risks without fear of punishment

How to build:
- Admit your mistakes
- Ask for input
- Acknowledge good ideas
- Don't punish failures (learn from them)
- Appreciate diverse perspectives
- Give direct feedback

Impact:
- Higher engagement
- More innovation
- Better problem-solving
- Higher retention

### Conflict Resolution

Types of conflict:
- Task conflict: Different ideas on approach (good)
- Process conflict: Disagreement on how to work (neutral)
- Relationship conflict: Personal friction (bad)

Resolution process:
1. Acknowledge the conflict
2. Get both perspectives
3. Find common ground
4. Explore options together
5. Agree on path forward
6. Follow up to ensure resolved

### Building Trust
- Do what you say
- Be transparent
- Listen actively
- Admit mistakes
- Give credit
- Consistent behavior
- Vulnerability

## DELEGATION

### Benefits
- Frees your time
- Develops team members
- Faster execution
- Better ideas
- Engagement increases

### How to Delegate

Step 1: Choose right person
- Can they do it?
- Will this develop them?
- Do they have capacity?

Step 2: Be clear
- What's the goal?
- What's the context?
- What's the deadline?
- What authority do they have?
- What's success?

Step 3: Support but don't hover
- Check in regularly
- Offer help but don't take over
- Let them struggle if learning
- Adjust if truly failing

Step 4: Feedback and recognition
- Acknowledge good work
- Constructive feedback on improvement
- Let them know impact

### Mistakes to avoid
- Not delegating (bottleneck)
- Delegating then taking over
- No clarity on expectations
- No feedback or recognition
- Delegating only bad tasks

## SCALING OPERATIONS

### Hiring
When to hire:
- Revenue supports salary
- Clear role and responsibility
- Proven need
- Have time to onboard

Hiring process:
- Define role clearly
- Screen resumes
- Phone interview
- Technical/skill assessment
- In-person interviews (multiple)
- Reference checks
- Offer and negotiation

Cultural fit:
- Mission alignment
- Value alignment
- Communication style
- Team compatibility
- Long-term potential

### Onboarding
Week 1:
- Welcome and orientation
- Equipment setup
- Team introductions
- First projects assigned

Week 2-4:
- Deep training
- Shadowing
- Independent work
- Regular check-ins

Month 3:
- Full productivity
- Company culture integrated
- Relationships built
- Success metrics defined

## FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT

### Key Metrics

Revenue:
- Total revenue
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Revenue growth rate

Costs:
- Fixed costs (salaries, rent)
- Variable costs (per unit production)
- Gross margin (Revenue - COGS)
- Operating expenses (everything)

Profitability:
- Gross profit: Revenue - COGS
- Net profit: Total revenue - all expenses
- Margin: Profit as percentage of revenue
- Breakeven: No profit or loss

### Budgeting

Annual Process:
1. Forecast revenue conservatively
2. Estimate expenses by category
3. Plan headcount and timing
4. Set departmental budgets
5. Track actuals monthly
6. Adjust quarterly as needed

Budget Categories:
- Headcount (usually largest)
- Technology and tools
- Marketing and customer acquisition
- Operations and overhead
- Contingency (10-15%)

### Cash Flow
Definition: When money actually comes in and goes out

Importance:
- More important than profitability
- Can be profitable but cash poor
- Timing matters

Management:
- Invoice quickly
- Collect quickly
- Pay strategically
- Forecast cash needs
- Maintain reserves

## CRISIS MANAGEMENT

### Crisis Types
- Product failure
- Key person departure
- Financial crisis
- Security breach
- Public relations issue
- Team conflict

### Crisis Response
1. Immediate: Assess and stabilize
2. Clear communication: Tell truth quickly
3. Take responsibility: Own the issue
4. Action plan: Here's how we fix it
5. Transparency: Keep stakeholders informed
6. Follow-up: Ensure it doesn't repeat

### Communication During Crisis
- Acknowledge quickly
- Facts over rumors
- Regular updates
- Transparent about challenges
- Show clear path forward
- Thank teams working on it
