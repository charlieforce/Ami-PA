## Why the Hybrid Model Works

**Traditional NGO Problem:** Donor-dependent, hard to scale
**Venture Capital Problem:** Profit-focused, abandons underserved markets
**Hybrid Solution:**
1. Nonprofit proves impact, attracts grants
2. For-profits solve real problems, generate revenue
3. Revenue funds nonprofit expansion
4. Impact validates product-market fit
5. Virtuous cycle: More impact → More revenue → More impact

**Charlie's bet:** Profit and purpose can be perfectly aligned.
