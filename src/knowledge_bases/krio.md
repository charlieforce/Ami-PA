# KRIO LANGUAGE EXPERTISE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## FUNDAMENTALS

### What is Krio?
Krio is the lingua franca of Sierra Leone, spoken by millions
Language family: English-based Creole with African influences (Yoruba, Temne, Mende, Portuguese)
Heritage: Born from Nova Scotian settlers, liberated Africans, and indigenous peoples
Modern usage: Official language, spoken across all ethnic groups
Global: Diaspora communities worldwide speak Krio

### Characteristics
- English vocabulary base with African grammar
- Tonal elements (context changes meaning)
- Flexible word order (more flexible than English)
- Expressive and poetic
- Rich in proverbs and idioms
- Living language (evolves with time)

## KRIO GRAMMAR

### Subject-Verb Structure
Simpler than English, no complex conjugations

Ah go come = I will come (future)
Ah come = I came (past)
Ah di come = I'm coming (present continuous)
Ah go come = I will come

Dem go come = They will come
Di ting go work = The thing will work
We don come = We have come

### Possession
My = My
Your = Your
Am = His/Her
We/Ours = Our
Dem/Theirs = Their

Example:
My family = My family
Your house = Your house
Am pikin = His child
We salone = Our Sierra Leone
Dem business = Their business

### Questions
Wetin? = What?
Who? = Who?
Where? = Where?
When? = When?
Why? = Why?
How? = How?
Which one? = Which one?

Ah go come na? = Will I come? (Is it that I will come?)
Di ting work na? = Does it work?

## TIPS FOR SPEAKING KRIO

### Pronunciation
- Rhythm matters: Krio has musical flow
- Tones: Rising tone at end of statement can change meaning
- Consonant sounds: Similar to English but softer
- Vowels: Pronounced clearly

### Cultural Context
- Respect elders: Use formal address
- Humor: Self-deprecating and playful
- Directness: Often more direct than English
- Community: Emphasis on collective (we vs I)
- Story-telling: Narratives are valued

### Natural Speech
- Use gestures: Krio is expressive
- Tone of voice: Conveys emotion
- Repetition: Emphasis through repetition ("fine fine", "true true")
- Questions: Common conversational style
- Proverbs: Add depth and wisdom

### Learning Journey
- Listen to music: Krio music for pronunciation
- Watch films: Salone films and content
- Speak with natives: Real conversation is best
- Practice proverbs: Part of cultural knowledge
- Embrace mistakes: Natural learning path
- Ask questions: People love teaching language
