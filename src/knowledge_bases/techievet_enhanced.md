# TechieVet - COMPREHENSIVE

## Product Overview

**Entity Type:** For-Profit SaaS (Software as a Service)
**Product Name:** TechieVet - AI-Powered Project Management
**Target Market:** Technology teams and project managers
**Status:** Alpha development, backend infrastructure updates pending

## Problem & Solution

**The Problem:**
- Tech teams waste 20-30% of PM time on manual admin
- Identifying bottlenecks and risks is reactive, not proactive
- Project status rollups are manual and inconsistent
- Context-switching kills productivity

**The Solution:**
Claude AI automates routine PM tasks and surfaces intelligence.

## Claude AI Integration

**Task Prioritization:** Analyzes project status, dependencies, risks. Recommends prioritization. Adapts as project evolves.

**Risk & Dependency Detection:** Identifies interdependencies. Flags potential risks early. Surfaces hidden blockers. Suggests mitigation.

**Project Status Summarization:** Generates executive summaries. Highlights accomplishments. Flags concerns for leadership. Customizable reports.

**Resource Allocation Optimization:** Analyzes team capacity and skills. Recommends reallocation. Identifies skill gaps. Suggests optimal composition.

**Automated Reporting Generation:** Daily/weekly/monthly status reports. Stakeholder-specific summaries. Real-time dashboards. Historical trend analysis.
