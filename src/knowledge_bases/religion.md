# RELIGION EXPERTISE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## MAJOR WORLD RELIGIONS

### Christianity

Beliefs:
- God revealed through Jesus Christ
- Salvation through faith in Jesus
- Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Spirit)
- Resurrection of Jesus
- Eternal life through Christ

Major denominations:
- Catholic: Pope, traditions, Mary veneration
- Protestant: Bible-based, varied practices
- Orthodox: Liturgical, icon veneration
- Pentecostal/Charismatic: Holy Spirit emphasis, speaking in tongues
- Others: Methodist, Baptist, Adventist, etc.

Practices:
- Prayer and meditation
- Church attendance
- Sacraments/Communion
- Bible reading
- Charitable works
- Worship through music and song

Holy days:
- Christmas (Jesus birth)
- Easter (Jesus resurrection)
- Pentecost (Holy Spirit)
- Various saint days

---

### Islam

Beliefs:
- One God (Allah)
- Prophet Muhammad (final messenger)
- Quran (holy book)
- Five Pillars (core practices)
- Day of judgment
- Heaven and hell

Five Pillars (core practices):
1. Declaration of faith (Shahada)
2. Prayer (Salah) - 5 times daily
3. Charity (Zakat) - to poor
4. Fasting (Sawm) - Ramadan
5. Pilgrimage (Hajj) - to Mecca (if able)

Major divisions:
- Sunni: Majority, various schools of law
- Shia: Imam leadership, different practices
- Sufi: Mystical, spiritual focus

Practices:
- Daily prayers (facing Mecca)
- Quran recitation
- Ablution/purification
- Modesty in dress
- Halal diet
- Avoiding alcohol/pork

Holy periods:
- Ramadan (fasting month)
- Eid al-Fitr (end of Ramadan)
- Eid al-Adha (festival of sacrifice)
- Islamic New Year
- Prophet's birthday

---

### Buddhism

Beliefs:
- Buddha (Siddhartha Gautama) as teacher
- Four Noble Truths (suffering and path to end it)
- Eightfold Path (right living)
- Cycle of birth/death/rebirth
- Enlightenment as goal
- No central god figure

Core concepts:
- Karma: Actions have consequences
- Nirvana: Liberation from suffering
- Impermanence: Everything changes
- Non-self: No permanent soul
- Mindfulness: Present awareness

Major branches:
- Theravada: Earliest, individual path
- Mahayana: Broader salvation
- Vajrayana: Tibetan Buddhism, ritual focus

Practices:
- Meditation (central practice)
- Mindfulness
- Ethical living
- Study of teachings
- Monastic communities
- Pilgrimage to sacred sites

Holy days:
- Vesak (Buddha's birthday)
- Bodhi Day (enlightenment day)
- Various traditions vary

---

### Hinduism

Beliefs:
- Multiple gods manifestations of ultimate reality (Brahman)
- Cycle of birth/death/rebirth (samsara)
- Karma determines rebirth
- Dharma (duty/righteousness)
- Multiple paths to enlightenment
- Ancient texts (Vedas, Upanishads)

Main gods:
- Brahma (creator)
- Vishnu (preserver)
- Shiva (destroyer/transformer)
- Shakti (feminine divine energy)

Practices:
- Puja (worship/prayer)
- Meditation and yoga
- Study of texts
- Pilgrimage to sacred sites
- Dietary practices (many vegetarian)
- Rituals and ceremonies
- Honoring elders and teachers

Holy days:
- Diwali (festival of lights)
- Holi (spring festival)
- Navaratri (nine nights, goddess)
- Various regional celebrations

---

### Judaism

Beliefs:
- One God (Yahweh)
- Torah (first five books of Bible)
- Covenant between God and Jewish people
- Messiah coming
- Ethical living emphasis
- Community and tradition

Practices:
- Prayer (especially Sabbath)
- Keeping kosher (dietary laws)
- Sabbath observance (Friday-Saturday)
- Torah study
- Charitable giving
- Celebrating festivals
- Family rituals

Holy days:
- Shabbat (weekly Sabbath)
- Passover (liberation from Egypt)
- Shavuot (giving of Torah)
- Rosh Hashanah (new year)
- Yom Kippur (day of atonement)
- Sukkot (harvest festival)
- Hanukkah (lights, rededication)

## COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS

### Key Differences

View of God:
- Christianity/Judaism/Islam: One God
- Hinduism: Multiple gods/one ultimate reality
- Buddhism: No god figure, teacher-focused

Salvation/Liberation:
- Christianity: Through Jesus Christ
- Islam: Through submission to Allah
- Judaism: Following Torah
- Hinduism: Through knowledge and karma
- Buddhism: Through enlightenment

Scriptures:
- Christianity: Bible
- Islam: Quran
- Judaism: Torah and Talmud
- Hinduism: Vedas, Upanishads, epics
- Buddhism: Sutras and teacher lineages

- Community belonging

- Celebration of faith

## RELIGION IN AFRICA CONTEXT

### Sierra Leone

Religious makeup:
- Muslim majority (60%)
- Christian minority (30%)
- Traditional religions (10%)
- High interfaith cooperation
- Syncretism (mixing practices)

Characteristics:
- Respect for all faiths
- Shared celebrations
- Interfaith marriages common
- Religious harmony strong
- Community-oriented faith practice

---

### Kenya

Religious makeup:
- Christian majority (80%+)
- Muslim minority (8-10%)
- Traditional religions
- Hindu, Buddhist, other small percentages
- Growing evangelical Christianity

## PRACTICAL RELIGIOUS AWARENESS

Understanding:
- Prayer times (impacts schedules)
- Dietary practices (food business)
- Sabbath observance (work schedules)
- Fasting periods (Ramadan, Lent)
- Religious holidays
- Modesty norms
- Ritual requirements

- Accommodate holy days
- Respect dietary needs
- Honor gender dynamics
- Allow religious expression
- Inclusive communication

---

### For Personal Relationships

Understanding:
- Their faith's importance
- Practices and requirements
- Values and ethics
- Taboos and restrictions
- Community and family role
- Spiritual perspective on life

Respect:
- Ask questions respectfully
- Don't mock or demean
- Attend celebrations if invited
- Respect religious objects
- Support their practice
- Find common ground

## CRITIQUES & CHALLENGES

- Political/power structures

- Wealth inequality
- Abuse of authority

Between religions:
- Historical conflicts
- Territorial disputes
- Theological disagreements
- Competition for converts
- Fundamentalism
- Violence and terrorism

- Interfaith cooperation growing
