# GII Connect - COMPREHENSIVE

## Platform Overview

**Entity Type:** For-Profit EdTech Company
**Purpose:** Commercial digital learning platform + revenue for GII nonprofit
**Business Model:** NGO white-label licensing + direct subscriptions
**Status:** Beta phase, commercial launch preparation

## AI-Powered Multilingual Tutor

### Languages Supported
- **Currently Live:** English, Krio (Sierra Leone), Luo (Kenya), Swahili (East Africa)
- **In Development:** Mende, Temne (Sierra Leone)
- **Future:** Amharic, Igbo, Yoruba, French

## Core Features

1. **AI-Powered Multilingual Tutor** - Personalized learning in native languages
2. **Mentorship System** - Peer and professional mentor matching
3. **AI-Matched Opportunity Hub** - Job, freelance, internship, funding matching
4. **Learning Pathways** - Tech, entrepreneurship, freelancing, AI/ML, coding
5. **Community Features** - Forums, cohort courses, peer support, networking

## Commercial Strategy

**Primary Revenue:** NGO white-label licensing
- Educational NGOs license platform
- Customize branding and curriculum
- GII Connect handles technology
- NGO focuses on pedagogy
- Revenue: Licensing fee + per-learner payments

**Secondary Revenue:**
- Individual course subscriptions
- Organizational subscriptions
- Mentor membership fees
- Opportunity hub premium access
