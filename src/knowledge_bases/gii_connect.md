# GII Connect
## EdTech Platform for African Tech Education

### What Is GII Connect?
GII Connect is a **for-profit complementary platform** to the nonprofit GII. While nonprofit GII delivers free education in Africa, GII Connect provides:
- Scalable digital learning products
- AI-powered personalized tutoring
- Commercial licensing to organizations
- Revenue to fund GII's expansion

**Website:** giiconnect.org or giiconnect.africa (in development)

### The Strategic Model
**Nonprofit (GII):** Free education, maximum reach, community impact
**For-Profit (GII Connect):** Scalable digital products, sustainable revenue, commercial licensing

This hybrid model allows GII to scale free education while generating revenue through commercial channels.

### Core Platform Features

**AI-Powered Multilingual Tutor**
Supports African languages for localized learning:
- **Krio** (Sierra Leone)
- **Mende** (Sierra Leone)
- **Temne** (Sierra Leone)
- **Luo** (Kenya)
- **Swahili** (Kenya & East Africa)

This is crucial because education in local languages increases comprehension by 40-60% and reduces dropout rates.

**Mentorship System**
- Peer-to-peer mentorship matching
- Professional mentor connections
- Accountability and progress tracking
- Real-world guidance from successful founders

**AI-Matched Opportunity Hub**
- Intelligent matching between learners and opportunities
- Job/freelance work discovery
- Investment opportunities
- Partnership matching
- Skills-based recommendations

**Learning Paths**
- Tech entrepreneurship courses
- Product management training
- Freelancing fundamentals
- Coding and AI skills
- Business planning

### Technical Stack

**Frontend:**
- React + Vite (fast, lightweight bundling)
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Managed by Jackson (React/Vite specialist)

**Backend:**
- Node.js + Express (scalable JavaScript API)
- Managed by Nelson (backend architect)

**Database:**
- PostgreSQL via Supabase (serverless scaling)
- Reliable, scalable, cost-effective

**Deployment:**
- Railway (one-click deployment, minimal DevOps)
- Automatic scaling for traffic spikes

**Communications:**
- Africa's Talking SMS/Voice API
- SMS notifications for offline-first experience
- Voice services for low-literacy populations

**Media Handling:**
- Cloudinary for image optimization
- CDN delivery for fast loading
- Handles high-res images on low-bandwidth connections

**Design Philosophy:**
Offline-first, low-bandwidth, mobile-first - because African users have:
- Limited consistent internet
- Expensive data plans ($0.50-$2 per GB)
- Smartphones as primary device
- High latency on 3G/4G
