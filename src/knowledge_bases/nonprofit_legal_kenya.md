# NON PROFIT ORGANIZATION LEGAL REQUIREMENTS IN KENYA KNOWLEDGE BASE

## KENYAN LEGAL FRAMEWORK

### NGO Law in Kenya

Primary legislation:
- NGO Coordination Act 1990
- Societies Act (Cap 108)
- Public Benefit Organizations (PBO) Law
- Constitution of Kenya (Chapter 6 - governance)

Regulatory body:
- NGO Coordination Board (NCB)
- Ministry of Interior
- Office of the Attorney General

---

## REGISTRATION PROCESS IN KENYA

### Step 1: Incorporation as Trustee or Society

Option A: Register as Society
- Under Societies Act
- Simpler process
- Limited structure
- Registrar: County commissioner or national office

Option B: Register as Trust
- Trusts Act
- More formal
- Greater flexibility
- More expensive

Documents needed:
- Constitution/bylaws
- List of members
- Bank account details
- Addresses of leaders
- Proof of premises

Cost: 5,000-15,000 KES

Timeline: 2-4 weeks

---

### Step 2: NGO Coordination Board Registration

After incorporating, register with NGO Board

Documents:
- Certificate of incorporation
- Constitution
- Board members information
- Bank details
- Program description
- Funding sources
- Annual budget

Process:
1. Complete registration form
2. Submit with documents
3. NCB review
4. Payment of registration fee
5. Receipt of certificate

Cost: Varies (typically 10,000-50,000 KES)

Timeline: 4-8 weeks

---

### Step 3: Tax Exemption

PBO Status (Public Benefit Organization):
- Applies through Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)
- Exemption from corporate income tax
- VAT exemption
- Import duty exemption

Requirements:
- Registered with NGO Board
- Audited financial statements
- Program demonstrating public benefit
- Board composition requirements

Benefits:
- Tax-free operations
- Donor tax deductions (for donors)
- Credibility with donors

---

## GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENTS IN KENYA

### Board of Directors

Requirements:
- Minimum 3 directors
- Majority Kenyan citizens
- Independent members
- Diverse expertise
- No conflicts of interest

Responsibilities:
- Strategic direction
- Financial oversight
- Legal compliance
- Stakeholder accountability
- Annual meetings

---

## FINANCIAL COMPLIANCE IN KENYA

### Accounting Standards

Requirements:
- International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)
- Annual financial statements
- Regular audits (if revenue exceeds 1 million KES)
- Bank reconciliation

Reporting:
- Annual returns to NGO Board
- Financial transparency
- Donor reporting
- Public accountability

Accounts:
- Separate bank account
- No personal mixing
- Clear records
- Segregation of duties

---

## EMPLOYMENT LAW IN KENYA

### Staff Compliance

Requirements:
- Employment contracts
- PAYE tax withholding
- National Social Security Fund (NSSF)
- National Hospital Insurance Fund (NHIF)
- Workers' compensation insurance

Labor laws:
- Minimum wage compliance
- Working hours regulations
- Leave entitlements
- Safe workplace
- Non-discrimination

---

## ANNUAL COMPLIANCE CALENDAR FOR KENYA

### Key Deadlines

January-February:
- Audit of previous year
- Board strategic planning
- Annual general meeting

March-April:
- File annual return to NGO Board
- Tax compliance with KRA
- PBO renewal if applicable

Throughout year:
- Monthly bank reconciliation
- Quarterly financial reviews
- Quarterly board meetings
- Monthly payroll compliance

June-August:
- Mid-year financial review
- Program evaluation
- Donor reporting

September-December:
- Year-end accounting
- Audit preparation
- Budget for next year
- Annual planning

Compliance:
- Audit costs high

## COSTS FOR KENYAN NGO

### Startup Costs

Registration:
- Incorporation: 10,000-20,000 KES
- NGO Board: 10,000-50,000 KES
- PBO Status: 10,000-30,000 KES
- Lawyer fees: 50,000-200,000 KES

Total startup: 80,000-300,000 KES (approximately 600-2,300 USD)

### Annual Costs

- Audit: 50,000-200,000 KES (depending on size)
- Accounting: 20,000-50,000 KES
- Compliance: 10,000-30,000 KES
- Renewal fees: Various

Total annual: 80,000-280,000 KES (600-2,100 USD)
