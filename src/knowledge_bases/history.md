# HISTORY ENGINE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## SIERRA LEONE HISTORY

### Pre-Colonial (Pre-1462)
- Indigenous kingdoms: Temne, Mende, Limba, Bullom, Sherbro, Lokko
- Cultural practices: Poro, Sande societies
- Trade routes
- Spiritual traditions

### Portuguese Period (1462-1600s)
- Pedro de Sintra naming Sierra Leone
- Trade establishments
- Slave trade beginning

### Colonial Period (1600s-1960)
- British colonization
- Freetown as freed slave settlement
- Thomas Peters and Nova Scotians (1792)
- Jamaican Maroons (1800)
- Liberated Africans/Recaptives
- Cotton Tree significance
- Fourah Bay College (1827) - First African university
- Colonial administration

### Independence Era (1961)
- Sir Milton Margai
- Post-independence politics
- Development challenges
- Civil war (1991-2002)

### Modern Era (2002-Present)
- Post-conflict reconstruction
- Ebola outbreak (2014-2016)
- Economic development
- Diaspora connections

## KRIO CULTURE & LANGUAGE

### Krio Origins
- Blend of English, African languages, Caribbean influences
- Developed from multilingual contact in Freetown
- Native language of Krio people

### Cultural Practices
- Naming ceremonies
- Wedding traditions
- Funeral rites
- Food culture (cassava leaf, plasas, groundnut soup)
- Music (gumbe, palm wine music)
- Architecture (board houses)
